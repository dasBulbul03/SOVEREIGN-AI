# Pump Maintenance and Isolation SOP

## Safe isolation

Before maintenance, stop pump P-101 from the local control panel. Close suction valve V-14, then close discharge valve V-15. Apply lockout/tagout to the motor disconnect and both isolation valves. Open drain valve DV-3 slowly and verify the pressure gauge reads zero bar. The responsible technician and area operator must both sign the isolation certificate before the casing is opened.

## Restart

Confirm guards are installed, DV-3 is closed, and tools are cleared. Remove lockout/tagout under the two-person authorization rule. Open V-14 fully, vent trapped air, then open V-15 to 25 percent. Start P-101 and inspect for vibration or leakage before gradually opening V-15 fully.

## Emergency rule

If pressure does not fall to zero, stop work. Do not loosen flanges or attempt maintenance. Escalate to the shift supervisor and verify the valve lineup against the piping and instrumentation diagram.
